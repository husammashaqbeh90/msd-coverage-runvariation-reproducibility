# Figure typesetting requirements

| Figure | Drawn width | Required placement | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Study design | 7.1 in | full page / two-column | flow diagram with explicit exclusions and two analysis arms |
| 2 Coverage-conditioned ATE | 7.1 in | full page / two-column | two panels side by side |
| 3 Repeated-run variation | 7.1 in | full page / two-column | ratio distributions and threshold-crossing triplets |
| S1 Complete repeated-run ranges | 7.1 in × 6.4 in | **full page / two-column, must not be reduced into a single column** | 59 + 63 sequence labels at 5.2 pt; single-column reduction makes them illegible |

All four are supplied as vector PDF (preferred for submission) and 300 dpi PNG.
Fonts are embedded (`pdf.fonttype 42`). Category identity is encoded by marker
shape and fill as well as tone, so the figures remain readable in greyscale and
under colour-vision deficiency.
